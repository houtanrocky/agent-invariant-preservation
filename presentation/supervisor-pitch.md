# Supervisor pitch

I am Houtan Rocky, beginning an MSc. I propose a bounded empirical study of whether coding agents can
implement backend feature changes while preserving transactional state requirements. Feature examples
alone may miss retry, rollback or concurrency problems; this is the motivation, not an observed finding.

The planned comparison gives both conditions the same requirements, with one additionally prompting
invariant-oriented reasoning. Independent hidden evaluations and human-reviewed references aim to make
the measurements credible. The four-week ceiling is eight tasks and 96 main runs, pending pilot feasibility.

Current evidence: working research infrastructure and draft methods; no validated subject tasks or agent
results. Closest overlap to review is PGS/LISA, and novelty remains tentative. I seek feedback on the gap,
two concrete tasks, oracle quality and an achievable scope, with a reproducible report as the deliverable.
Next: bring a pinned-code invariant map and pilot evidence, not unsupported claims of success.
