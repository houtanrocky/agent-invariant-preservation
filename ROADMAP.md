# Four-week roadmap

Timebox: four weeks from the researcher's chosen start date; calendar dates are not assumed.
Track actual time and reasoning in progress/daily-log.md. Consult the detailed weekly pages.

| Week | Goal | Exit gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Literature, pinned baseline, invariant map, two tasks, first dry run | Two genuinely validated tasks and reproducible baseline, or documented scope revision |
| 2 | Complete benchmark and verify execution | All selected tasks reviewed; A/B parity checked; protocol and schedule frozen |
| 3 | Collect and review | Every planned attempt accounted for, traces retained, outcomes independently checked |
| 4 | Analyze and communicate | Regenerable tables/figures, cautious paper, reproducibility audit and supervisor presentation |

The 8-task/96-run plan is a ceiling. If validation cannot fit, shrink transparently before main collection.
Allow time for a second human review and database failures; do not spend week 4 constructing missing oracles.
No adverse or null result blocks completion of an honest report.

First-day actions: follow docs/getting-started.md and progress/week-01.md. Stop main-study collection
until baseline, task, isolation and protocol gates are satisfied. Revisit feasibility after the pilot.
