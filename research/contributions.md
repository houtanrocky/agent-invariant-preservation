# Intended contributions

1. A small collection of human-reviewed transactional maintenance tasks with independently checked oracles.
2. A controlled comparison of two prompt conditions, with complete component outcomes and provenance.
3. A trace-grounded taxonomy of failures and an openly documented reproducibility workflow.

These are goals. The current contribution is infrastructure and a draft protocol, not study evidence.
Task coverage, oracle strength and honest negative results matter more than a favorable intervention effect.
