# Scope and limitations

The initial project lasts at most four weeks. One Go marketplace backend, at most eight validated changes,
two coding-agent systems, two conditions and three repetitions per cell define the provisional ceiling.
No generalization to all languages, databases, models or production finance systems is warranted.

Authentication, distributed deployment, throughput benchmarking and formal verification are outside
initial scope. Concurrency tests sample selected histories and cannot prove absence of anomalies.
Correctness means passing the frozen oracles, with human audit of suspicious successes.

Next: use a week-2 feasibility gate. Reduce tasks or repetitions transparently before main runs if time
is insufficient; preserve an interpretable matched comparison and disclose selection effects.
