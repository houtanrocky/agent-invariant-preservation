# Ethics and research integrity

Use synthetic domain fixtures only; no real financial records or private user data are required.
Observe provider terms and document model/data retention settings. Credentials remain local and ignored.
Track human reference work separately from agent patches; record AI assistance to infrastructure and writing.

Keep every attempted run, including failures, cancellations and environment incidents. Corrections append
a new record referencing the original, with rationale and reviewer; do not silently overwrite raw evidence.
Freeze criteria before main outcomes and mark later changes exploratory. A null or unfavorable result is valid.

Publish neither secrets nor hidden tests while data collection is in progress. Preserve a private original
before making a redacted trace; record both checksums with restricted access to the original.
Institutional ethics requirements and licensing permissions remain researcher decisions to verify.
