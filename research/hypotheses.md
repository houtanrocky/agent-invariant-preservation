# Preliminary hypotheses

H1: Explicit invariant-oriented guidance increases the probability of joint task success relative to
standard guidance within the selected task and agent setting. This is an untested directional hypothesis.
H0 for a planned comparison: the relevant success difference is zero. Report the estimated difference
and uncertainty even if no hypothesis test is defensible. Negative differences must also be reported.

The estimand is the mean B−A success-probability difference with equal weighting across validated tasks
and selected systems. It is conditional on the final benchmark, model versions and budgets.
Task-specific and system-specific effects may differ. Resource associations are exploratory and
are not preregistered directional hypotheses.

Next: after pilot feasibility assessment, freeze estimand, inferential method, exclusions and minimum
effect of interest in the preregistration. Never revise H1 to fit main-study outcomes.
