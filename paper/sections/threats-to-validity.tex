\section{Threats to Validity}
Construct validity depends on oracle adequacy and whether the tasks represent meaningful changes.
References and rejected defects provide evidence but cannot prove completeness. Internal validity
depends on information parity, independent sessions, balanced execution order and stable environments.
The researcher constructs tasks and evaluates outcomes, motivating independent human review.

A small purposive sample from one backend limits external validity. Two systems cannot characterize
all coding agents, and public code may have been present in model training. Repeated runs are dependent
through task and system; few tasks, sparse failures and multiple comparisons constrain inference.
Concurrency schedules sample behaviors rather than exhaust them. Provider drift and incomplete resource
reporting may impair exact regeneration. Report residual limitations and realized protocol deviations.
